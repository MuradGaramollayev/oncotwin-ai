Evidence base
Brandsma et al. (2008) — Lancet Oncology
Systematic review | GBM n=592
Pseudoprogression occurs in up to 30% of GBM patients post-RT/TMZ, predominantly within 3 months of completing radiotherapy. MGMT methylated patients show significantly higher rates (61% vs 15% unmethylated).
MGMT meth: OR 3.8 (p<0.001)
Hygino da Cruz et al. (2011) — AJNR
Prospective | n=147 | Advanced MRI
MR perfusion (rCBV) and MR spectroscopy most accurate non-invasive differentiators. rCBV threshold >2.0 favours true progression (sensitivity 88%, specificity 72%). Early follow-up imaging at 6–8 weeks recommended when indeterminate.
rCBV >2.0: Sens 88% / Spec 72%
